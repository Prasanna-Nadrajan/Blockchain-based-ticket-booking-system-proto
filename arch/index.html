The ticketing industry faces severe challenges:

  • Counterfeiting & Scalping: High-resolution printers replicate barcodes in minutes — standard scanners cannot detect fakes
  • Centralized System Failures: Siloed databases provide zero transparency into ticket transfer history
  • Bot Networks: Sweep inventory within seconds of release, inflating resale prices by 300–1000%
  • No On-Chain Provenance: Screenshot-based QR codes allow unlimited copies to be shared and reused
  • Single Point of Failure: If the ticketing platform's DB is breached, all tickets are compromised

The ticketing industry demands a trustless, decentralized solution — the status quo is broken.

The Solution — Why Blockchain?

❌ Centralized (Old Way)

  • Single corporate database
  • Static barcodes easily copied
  • Zero resale transparency
  • Buyer identity unverifiable

✅ BlockTicket (New Way)

  • ERC-721 NFTs on Ethereum/Polygon
  • Time-based cryptographic QR codes
  • On-chain resale caps & creator royalties
  • Wallet-based identity

Trustless, transparent, programmable, and censorship-resistant by design.
